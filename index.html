My Top Spotify Artist


Yeng Constantino


Yeng Constantino is a Filipino singer and songwriter known for her powerful voice and emotional performances. She rose to fame in 2006 after winning the inaugural season of ABS-CBN's reality talent competition Pinoy Dream Academy. She is know as the Philippines' "Pop-Rock Princess" and "Pop-Rock Royalty". Yeng is celebrated for her expressive vocals, relatable songwriting, and incredible stage presence. She has released several successful albums and is considered one of the leading figures in the Philippine music industry.
